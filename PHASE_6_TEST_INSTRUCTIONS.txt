🚀 STEP 4: PREPARE TOKENS

Admin JWT (for /api/v1 read-only customer routes):

  curl -X POST http://localhost:8081/auth/login \
    -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
    -d '{"email": "admin@example.com", "password": "password123"}'

  TOKEN="<PASTE_THE_ACCESS_TOKEN_HERE>"

Internal service token (for /internal/v1 customer & cart routes).
This is the INTERNAL_SERVICE_TOKEN from apps/api/.env — apps/ai uses it.
It is sent in the "X-Service-Token" header.

  SERVICE_TOKEN="<PASTE_INTERNAL_SERVICE_TOKEN_FROM_ENV>"

You also need a variant id from Phase 5:

  VARIANT_ID="<PASTE_AN_ACTIVE_VARIANT_ID>"
